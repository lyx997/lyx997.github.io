What BirdCull does.

BirdCull focuses on practical first-pass review for bird photo sessions: similar burst frames, visible head and eye details, sharpness, pose, and export into familiar editing tools.

Bird-focused ranking

Scores images around bird photography review signals instead of treating every photo like a generic image.

Workflow output

Creates review folders or XMP star-rating sidecars for Lightroom, Bridge, Photo Mechanic, and similar tools.

Private local processing

Selected photos are processed on your computer. Payment details are handled by Paddle checkout.
